# CV and profile editing

These rules apply to the CV in `cv/` and the profile site in `me/`.

- English is the primary version. Use short sentences and plain English;
  use B2 vocabulary and technical terms where they make the meaning more precise.
  Keep the correct technical terms and product names.
- List English and Russian in the Languages section without proficiency labels.
- The candidate's current location is Belgrade, Serbia.
- At Restream Media, the candidate was a Senior Java Developer from 2016 to 2021
  and has combined Team Lead and Tech Lead responsibilities since 2021.
  Use year-only dates; do not invent months or assign undated achievements to a role.
- The Restream role has been fully remote since 2022. Keep that date separate from
  the promotion to Team Lead / Tech Lead in 2021.
- Keep the Russian version and the profile site consistent with the English CV.
- List Apache Kafka in the skills section without an experience qualifier.
  Do not add Kafka projects or production claims without confirmed details.
- Keep the scale figures separate: 10 million monthly users belongs to Wink;
  300 requests per second and 2 TB of data refer to each regional deployment.
- Add measured improvements only when they can be supported. Do not invent
  latency reductions, cost savings or availability figures.
- When preparing an application, adapt the wording and skill order to the job
  description without adding skills or experience the candidate does not have.
- Keep the PDF in a single-column layout with standard section names and selectable
  text. Do not reduce the font size to force the content onto one page.
- Build with pdfLaTeX and CM-Super to preserve the original Computer Modern fonts.
  Do not substitute fonts or switch PDF engines without a user request.
- After rebuilding, inspect both the PDF layout and extracted text. Check the section
  order, names, contacts, dates and technical keywords. Text extraction checks do not
  guarantee how a particular applicant tracking system will rank the CV.
- Update the published PDF files and the English CV thumbnail when changing the CV.

The ATS editing guidance follows [Harvard's recommendations](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/ai-resumes-and-cover-letters/):
use basic formatting, list skills explicitly and include relevant keywords in context.
